Admission Requirements

What You Need to Apply

Prepare the following documents to complete your application. Originals will be submitted upon enrollment.

1

Senior High School Card (SF9 / Form 138)

Original and photocopy of your latest report card with complete grades and GWA.

2

PSA-Issued Birth Certificate

Original PSA security paper copy of your birth certificate.

3

Certificate of Good Moral Character

Signed and sealed by your principal or guidance counselor from your last school.

4

2×2 ID Photos (4 pieces)

Recent color photos with white background, printed on photo paper.

5

Government-Issued ID

PhilSys, passport, UMID, or any valid government-issued identification.
